Your requests

Your IP address, URL, headers and data you send are processed to provide diagnostics and protect the service. The app keeps no persistent request history. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests in providing and securing the service (Article 6(1)(f)).

Logs & redaction

Request and message bodies are excluded from app logs. Common credential headers are always redacted in diagnostic logs. {{if not .Headers}}Request headers are not included in responses on this instance.{{else if .Unsafe}}Response credential redaction is disabled on this instance.{{else}}Common credential headers are also redacted in responses.{{end}} Redaction is not exhaustive; paths, queries and custom fields can still contain personal data.

Cookies & browser storage

The app sets no cookies and includes no analytics, ads or trackers. It saves only your chosen theme and curl/wget preference in this tab’s session storage. Browser session restoration can preserve those choices.

Fonts and assets load from this service. GitHub receives a request only when you follow an external link.

Public demo & your rights

Philip Schmid operates echo.container.education. Contact privacy@container.education for access, correction, deletion or other data rights. You may also complain to your data-protection authority. Other installations have their own operators.

Retention

The public demo disables routine request diagnostics. Infrastructure keeps operational logs for seven days; errors can include hostnames, paths and connection information. Aggregate metrics may remain for about 15 days. Request bodies and workbench history are not saved by the app.

Processing & protection

The public demo is hosted by Fly.io, Inc., with the application running in Germany. Hosting operations may involve technical data processing in the United States. We do not sell data or use it for advertising. Use HTTPS; privacy cannot be guaranteed.

More about data handling

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact privacy@container.education. We may need proportionate identity verification; we do not collect extra identifiers to locate diagnostic requests no longer retained. You may complain to the Swiss FDPIC or a competent EU supervisory authority.

Request and protection data

HTTP bodies are consumed and discarded after handling. WebSocket messages are echoed over the live connection; workbench activity stays in page memory. With protection enabled, peer-prefix rate limits are held in memory. Idle entries are removed during later checks after ten minutes; restart clears them. Durable quotas store only a UTC day and aggregate byte counts. Application metric labels contain no visitor IPs, header values or arbitrary request URLs.

We do not create visitor profiles or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Plain HTTP, raw TCP and plaintext gRPC are unencrypted; TLS protects data in transit but the service still receives what you send.

Recipients and transfers

Authorized operators and hosting, network and operational providers process data as needed to deliver and secure the demo. See Fly.io’s privacy policy and subprocessor list. Fly.io describes its commitments for covered EU–US and Swiss–US transfers in its Data Privacy Framework policy. Ask the privacy contact for recipient details and transfer safeguards.

Privacy correspondence

If you contact us, we use your email address and message to handle the request. We retain them only as needed to resolve it and meet applicable legal obligations.
